# Planner product ports

The seven Planner pages remain owned by `runtime/planner.ts`: Overview, OPEX, CAPEX, Headcount, Entities & allocations, FX rates and Versions. `plannerContribution` exports that domain and its original renderer. `projectPlanner`, `simulatePlannerCommand`, and `simulatePlannerCommands` use that same domain; previews execute against a copy and do not apply changes to the source snapshot. WP06 consumes the command adapter for import preview.

| Page | Domain and product coverage | Consumer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | Financial projection, monthly and capacity series, forecast insights and version differences | WP01 presents the projection; WP05 renders the series and contributions. |
| OPEX | Monthly drafts, annual/spread/reset, line actions and copied command previews | WP01 connects the monthly host; WP06 connects workbook preview. |
| CAPEX | Monthly drafts, annual/spread/reset, project actions and copied command previews | WP01 connects the monthly host; WP06 connects workbook preview. |
| Headcount | Positions, rates, synchronization, increase, leader domains and HR actual/forecast | WP01 connects the authorized headcount host. |
| Entities & allocations | Shared entity and allocation catalog and original domain commands | WP01 connects the authorized catalog host. |
| FX rates | Version-scoped rates and original domain commands | WP01 connects the authorized catalog host. |
| Versions | Original create, copy, edit, lock, unlock, official and delete commands; identity-based version comparison | WP01 retains the domain contribution and connects comparison. |

The table maps producer responsibilities; it does not certify that a consumer has been composed or tested. For acceptance, compare the base and two copies with budget 600000, actual 400000 and forecast 190000: replacing the 72000 item with 84000 and 78000 gives outlooks 602000 and 596000, against base 590000. Check both base delta and budget variance, then repeat under a restricted role and confirm the source and official version remain unchanged by previews.

`createPlannerProduct` binds the authenticated domain host, the installation's scoped source lifecycle and a coverage resolver. `issueSource(versionId, context)` issues a current actor-scoped selector in the returned evidence reference. `compareForecast` checks that source and actor before and after projection. Monthly budget, actual and outlook totals, original currency measures, USD conversions, planned FTE, HR budget and currency rates carry source evidence; missing values and rates stay absent. `plannerMonthlyPresentation` and `plannerCapacityPresentation` provide BI01 and BI04 series without recalculating money. `comparePlannerVersions` identifies matched, added, removed and incompatible components; it does not infer causes.

`plannerDifferencePresentation` publishes the endpoints and comparable component contributions for BI02/BI03, with reconciliation separate from cost coverage. `createPlannerForecastInsights` publishes the candidate's outlook-minus-budget margin, absent actuals, inherited budget and recorded responsible identities using current source evidence. `createPlannerObservedProjection` admits only confirmed actuals for a closed, non-future month and describes coverage for that metric, not the whole decision.

`createPlannerOverviewHost` resolves the selected baseline and candidate plus the current official version through the same authorized source issuer and Planner port. `plannerOverview` keeps change from the baseline, variance to the candidate budget and variance to the official budget as separate metrics. `renderPlannerOverview` renders that contribution without deriving a new financial model. `createPlannerVersionsHost` exposes the existing version lifecycle with closed requests, exact human intent, copied previews and retained conflict drafts. Their exports are ready for the WP01 composition; their presence is not proof that a route mounts them.

The Overview contribution opens each of its seven totals with native `details`/`summary` controls, then a record subtotal and its monthly components. Every level retains the baseline, candidate and official version references, selected period and projection currency. Budget details read the Planner's published `budget-usd` values with matching component identity, period, currency and evidence; outlook details use the authorized outlook components. Baseline changes and budget variances use `comparePlannerVersions` for the corresponding before/after measures. Opening a source shows its recorded measures in their declared currency and the source revision without copying Planner calculations or deriving a cause for a difference.

A structural addition or removal displays `Not present` on the missing side, while a present but unknown or invalid measure displays `Unavailable`; observed zero remains `0 USD`. A missing official version leaves its two metrics unavailable. Record subtotals never turn unknown months into zero, and partial records are not presented as a complete total. Each metric states whether its component composition is reconciled, incomplete, unavailable or unreconciled; reconciliation requires exact equality to the unchanged published total and does not remove coverage limitations. The renderer uses native disclosure state and escaped data attributes rather than global DOM IDs, preserving independent navigation when multiple Overviews are present. This is the WP03 HTML contribution and model; it does not establish that the shared WP01 screen mounts the contribution or satisfy the pending installed UI acceptance criteria.

For a classification registry, the source evidence identifies the physical Planner version, incarnation, revision, projection fields, period and digest. A comparison of that source with itself returns the current actor-filtered components as `<line-id>:<month>` with their monthly periods, USD amounts and source identities. Its values expose the matching original forecast, declared currency and converted outlook under `<line-id>:<month>:forecast`, `:currency-declared` and `:outlook-usd`. Component and value IDs are distinct from the top-level projection field names in the source evidence. These identities and values do not assert a decision category: an authorized classification must bind category and scope to them separately and be revalidated against the current source.

`prepareMonthlyDrafts` retains text and validation errors; `previewMonthlyDrafts` sends valid rows through the real domain on a copy and retains each conflict alongside its draft. `createPlannerMonthlyHost` authenticates the real UI request and actor, admits only existing visible rows, and checks the private human intent before its preview or transactional apply. Successful rows commit through the original commands and revisions; rejected rows retain their text and conflicts.

The real OPEX/CAPEX renderer mounts `renderMonthlyGrid` to expose saved monthly state, including absent values, explicit zero, inherited forecast, manual budget flags, closed months and the exact field permissions. The existing editing forms remain domain commands. The shared WP01 paste/retry client and the WP00 request contract require the proposed patches in `docs/sf2h/receipts/WP03`; those patches have not been applied outside this WP's ownership.

`createPlannerHeadcountHost` and `createPlannerCatalogHost` provide authorized read models and closed preview/apply actions for the existing domain. `plannerHrMonthState` qualifies each domain/month against only its active rate and FX dependencies: an independently recorded actual or forecast remains known when the budget rate is missing. Planned FTE remains distinct from actual availability. Owner/viewer receive the domain aggregates permitted by the pinned legacy policy, while individual compensation and Talent Review fields remain private. FX rates are version-scoped, entities and allocations are shared, and an allocation is a classification rather than a calculated split. Monthly, Headcount and catalog previews allocate deterministic temporary identities without consuming the host ID allocator or changing the original snapshot. They avoid live identities, legacy dictionary keys and revision-only tombstones; apply continues to use the real allocator. Catalog edits and deletes require exactly one matching target. A monthly egress failure reports reconciliation only when the transaction actually committed; rejected writes remain denied without claiming a durable change.

`plannerValueRef` identifies a monthly source measure. `createPlannerScenarioInputs` resolves it through an authorized current Planner projection. A host must supply real human confirmation evidence for confirmed assumptions; the default rejects confirmation. Scenario operations are previews and do not change the official version. Coverage for a modified scenario requires a case-specific reassessment, and the result remains conditioned while assumptions or inputs are unresolved.

`preparePlannerMutation` prepares a forecast edit on an existing OPEX or CAPEX line from an authenticated scoped source and a copied domain snapshot. Its mutation checks the same actor, source incarnation, source projection, month coverage, command revision and preview digest inside the eventual transaction. `plannerPreparedRevision` reads the exact post-write row revision. The composition host retains the private scope, operation ID and preview time, and WP11 supplies human confirmation, transactional application and receipt authority; preparation alone does not apply an edit. Synthetic HR rows require their own domain path and are not accepted by this producer.

`createPlannerPreparedReview` replays a prepared forecast in a copy under the current reviewer's authority, checks the current row revision and proves that the original and reviewer-scoped sources refer to the same physical record. It returns only the changed forecast fields, their currencies, source evidence and an optional authorized line label; the command remains private.

`createPlannerImportAnalysis` consumes a private C04 preview reader and a snapshot-level source fence supplied by the host. It replays the exact workbook command on a copy using the original preview identity, verifies the preview and command digests, and projects both sides through the same financial projection used by forecast comparison. The result identifies the C04 preview, marks candidate values hypothetical, stays conditioned and never applies the import.

`createStoredPlannerScenarioHost` resolves C08 results against the current authorized case and matches both request and result digests to exactly one current alternative. The WP11 case coverage resolver must be supplied through `createPlannerCoverageRouter`, with a current forecast coverage source for baseline references. `createPlannerResultPersistence` can persist scenario and comparison outputs in the case store; capability handlers return `outputRef` only after a successful authorized write. Comparison persistence requires the current scenario resolver so that it revalidates both alternatives before writing; a missing resolver returns unavailable. Scenario and comparison capability handlers also return unavailable when result persistence is not configured.

Comparison persistence recomputes the output and checks the case before and after writing. Nonempty criteria additionally require the real WP11 `comparisonBindings` and Planner replay; it compares current proofs, binds the stored comparison and reads that binding before returning `outputRef`. A failed binding after the C08 write leaves an auditable partial result and returns failure. Consumers must not treat a raw C08 read as a current criterion-backed comparison. The WP13 composition patch and recovery expectations are in the receipts directory.

`createPlannerConfirmationObserver` supports the `planner-c06-v1` financial summary scope and whole-month periods. Its fingerprint binds visible summary components and their internal FX, HR and shared-catalog dependencies; unsupported external dependencies remain unavailable. The observer and the historical-choice resimulation adapter provide a snapshot fence for the host to use with current authority. Resimulation does not change the recorded choice or by itself approve a new alternative.

`createPlannerCalculationEvidence` resolves a persisted scenario only with an immutable request binding, current C08 authority and two matching replays through the Planner. Its initial supported subset excludes assumptions and replacement references. All three injected readers and replay must reject nested evidence before invoking any resolver, including within coverage resolution; an ordinary C08 read or an unrestricted Planner is not suitable. The host must register the real request at the result producer and route the resulting calculation reference through C17; the issuer alone is not a consumer integration.

The issuer rejects reentrant runs, validates the complete record-only source closure including coverage evidence, clones callback inputs/outputs, and clears its recursion guard after failures. `createPlannerOperationVerifier` delegates admissibility to `simulatePlannerOperations` in an authenticated noncommitting Planner transaction, so closed months, duplicate targets, existing-value inclusion and invalid deferrals follow the original domain rules.

`comparePlannerPreview` compares an authorized current alternative with a transient Planner preview using only the baseline's real coverage reference. Its preview metric is conditioned and carries source evidence. Scenario and criterion observations are copied before the next resolver call, and preview requests, baseline observations and simulation results are retained independently of callback caches. A changed observation fails the final freshness check instead of silently replacing the baseline. `createPlannerSensitivityUnits` issues independent parameter and result units from the exact operation and actor-filtered source, then binds them to the single human-registered WP11 criterion rule. `createPlannerCriterionMetric` resolves that rule to the current domain metric; unknown criteria remain independent of financial completeness. Sensitivity sampling remains a separate WP13 producer injected through `PlannerPort.evaluateSensitivity`; the Planner factory reports it unavailable until that producer is connected. These exports do not by themselves prove a native, UI, import or BI entrypoint is connected; the composition and hosted checks must establish those consumers.

`createPlannerExactThreshold` supports a single direct USD replacement of an open non-HR month with finite components and a fixed baseline. The shared save, USD conversion, outlook and ordered subtotal operations are monotone, including floating-point plateaus; the solver verifies the neighboring representable value at the frontier. The result describes a conditioned metric, not complete cost coverage. Changes to those engine semantics require a new engine version and a fresh review of the proof.
