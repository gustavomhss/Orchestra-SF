# Executive Dashboard attention signals

The native Executive Dashboard shows every eligible attention signal returned by the current authorized source, in the source's order. Choosing a financial year or month does not hide an overdue action or an undated risk.

Use **Prioritize** beside a signal to move it into the first group. The source order is preserved within the prioritized and remaining groups. Use **Remove priority** to return it to the remaining group. These choices describe the view; they do not change the source record or its business priority.

The URL retains up to 32 source identifiers as presentation priorities, so reloading the page keeps the selection. A priority that is no longer present in the current authorized source does not display a stale record. The owner and source are checked again before a priority change is committed.

Use **Open record** to open a signal's native risk or Hot Topic record. The year and month follow the navigation. Opening the target requires its current read access and the exact original record ID. Contract renewal signals remain visible with their source reference; this view does not invent a native contract destination.
