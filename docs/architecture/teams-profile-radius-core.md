## Decisões e ordem da fronteira

- **identity:** Canonical SF context includes installation, tenant, corporate principal, selected Team and configuration/access/policy revisions; distinct explicit admin-without-Team/noTeams branches. Client-supplied values are selectors, never authority.
- **admission:** Corporate admission precedes Location/Project resolution, private SELECT/materialization, config import, process/network and mutation on SF paths, including compiled generic CLI endpoints.
- **cache:** Share only immutable config keyed by Team+config revision; principal credentials, grants, MCP clients, provider/account/plugin handles and callbacks are isolated/revalidated. Revocation closes stale handles and prevents late callbacks repopulating caches.
- **durable:** Sessions, meetings after detach, schedules, private outputs and recovery records carry durable owner independent of cwd/project/session guesses. Exact IDs/import/fork/move/retry and list/cursor filters are owner-checked before materialization/pagination.
- **resource-grants:** Team membership does not authorize every case/corpus/transcript/provider resource; installation resources and per-resource grants remain independent. Historical provenance bytes/IDs/digests are not rewritten into Team IDs.
- **migration-recovery:** Explicit old-owner mapping, quarantine ambiguous records, writer fence, atomic resumable cutover, immutable old migrations. Restore reconciles monotonic receipts/consumed slots/cancellations/issued IDs/high-water marks; uncertainty holds instead of redispatch.
- **technical:** Keep generic Project/Location/filesystem/repository utilities with positive technical tests, paired with negative SF tests proving they do not select profile or grant business access.

[packages/server/src/handlers/event.ts](https://github.com/gustavomhss/Orchestra-SF/blob/687759276c2c9121c2cfd20a5994b277ceb7ee4d/packages/server/src/handlers/event.ts) consome bus global e memoiza payload. Sem ledger suficiente, hold; nenhum efeito consumido volta a executar. Esses cenários são **novos requisitos**, não propriedades provadas do pin.
